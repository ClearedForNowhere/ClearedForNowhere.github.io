My Kingdom For A Sky #1 is the first series in a collection of photographs built around three things I’ve been passionate about for a long time: the sky, aviation, and photography.

The idea is simply to share, throughout each series, the photos that catch my eye and that I feel like keeping: an aircraft in the sky, a particular kind of light, or sometimes just a snapshot of everyday life.

This first series marks the beginning of My Kingdom For A Sky.
